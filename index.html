Interactive Web Portal for Aspiring Entrepreneurs

Many aspiring entrepreneurs find it hard to go from an idea to a real business. They have little access to mentors, startup knowledge and useful resources. This web portal gives them one place to learn, get ideas, find mentors and connect with other entrepreneurs.

End-User
  • Sign up and sign in
  • Search business categories
  • Search mentors and see their full profile
  • View business ideas, templates, market research, startup plans and financial support
  • Give feedback to the agent
Entrepreneur / Agent
  • Register with a form and wait for approval
  • After approval, log in and upload ideas, templates and opportunities
  • Discussion forum and private messaging
  • Share contacts with users
Admin
  • Log in and check agent profiles
  • Approve or reject agents
  • Give username and password to agents
  • Upload, edit and delete content, tutorials and events
  • Give feedback to agents
